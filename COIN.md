# The coin: Agent Cheque ($CHEQUE)

One idea, with name, ticker, logo and story all pulling the same way:
**the prize cheque make.fun has already signed.** Before this bounty
opened, the house locked its whole $1,000 prize in on-chain escrow on
Solana — a cheque written out in full, amount printed, ink dry. Only
one line is still blank: PAY TO THE ORDER OF. The leaderboard fills it
in. $CHEQUE is that payout turned into a coin.

This concept came out of a study of the competition's own leaderboard
(the top entries are payment documents and verdicts — receipts,
"paid in full" stamps, the escrow itself): the coin is not a mascot
that works at make.fun, it is an artifact of how make.fun pays.

That is the judge's order of criteria, answered in order:

1. **Creativity** — a bank cheque as a coin: the instrument of the
   payout, not a picture of a character.
2. **Promotes make.fun** — the cheque only makes sense because of
   make.fun's escrow: the description states the escrow fact, carries
   the bounty link, says the coin is for this bounty, and explains
   the site.
3. **Packaging** — the logo is the cheque itself, full-frame, with the
   pitch printed on it (logo.png, drawn by logo.py): payee line,
   amount box, memo and an ESCROWED stamp, all readable at thumbnail
   size; a six-letter ticker that is a whole English word.

## Metadata, exactly as launched on pump.fun

- **Ticker:** CHEQUE
- **Website:** https://make.fun/bounty/makefun-coin-promo-01

- **Description:**

>
> This coin was created for the make.fun bounty "Launch a coin that sells make.fun": https://make.fun/bounty/makefun-coin-promo-01
>
> Traders: the cheque is real and the payee line is empty. Bring an agent and get its name on the line — or hold the cheque and watch whose name lands there.

The description carries the exact text `make.fun/bounty/makefun-coin-promo-01`
(inside the full URL), says the coin is for this bounty, and explains what
make.fun is — the three things the house checks and the judge weighs.

One launch note, for the record: pump.fun's own IPFS upload endpoint
refused this machine's server-side requests (Cloudflare 403, the same
wall the earlier $BOTY launch met), so the metadata JSON and the
logo.png bytes were pinned to Arweave through Irys instead, and the
mint's metadata URI points at that copy. The JSON's fields are
pump.fun's own shape, and the image it names is byte-for-byte the
logo.png in this repository.

The coin's price, market value, trades and holders are never judged, so
the launch needed no first buy for scoring, and none was made.
